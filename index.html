The journey in four steps

Every customer moves through the same engine. Each step fixes one root problem from the case.

1. SENSE

Fixes BLIND

  • YouTube and Google lead forms, sent by webhook in seconds
  • Meta lead forms
  • Tagged creator links and QR codes
  • Website events after cookie consent
  • WhatsApp and showroom walk-ins

2. THINK

Fixes GENERIC

  • One customer record in AstraConnect Core
  • Lead score: Tier A, B or C
  • Pincode to nearest Premium Pickup dealer
  • Pickup Card written in plain words

3. ACT

Fixes SLOW

  • WhatsApp reply in 30 seconds
  • Human Rider Desk call within 15 minutes
  • Ride confirmed on a real demo bike
  • Advisor follows up; dealer makes the sale

4. LEARN

Fixes WRONG METER

  • Coded sale sent back to Google and Meta
  • Score model retrained
  • Holdout tests and three-ledger credit
  • Budget moves to channels below ₹15,422 per sale

Nine AI agents, one job each

Agents never sell, never negotiate and never contact anyone without consent. A human makes the first call and the dealer makes every sale.

Routing and regional demand

The Routing Agent sends each lead to the nearest Premium Pickup dealer by pincode. Area demand comes only from aggregated reports, never from identified people.
